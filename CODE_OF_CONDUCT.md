# Code of conduct

Panodash contributors, maintainers, and community participants are expected to make collaboration welcoming, respectful, and safe.

Be considerate; critique ideas and code rather than people; respect privacy and boundaries; give constructive feedback; and assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's contact information. Reports will be handled with discretion, with conflicts of interest recused where possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
