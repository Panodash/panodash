## What is this?

You check Stripe. Then Analytics. Then GitHub. Then your bank. Every morning. Nine tabs, every day, forever.

**Panodash ends the tab ritual.** It's a dashboard that lives where you already look — your browser's new tab — and shows every metric you care about the moment you open a window. Revenue, traffic, stars, subscribers, sleep score. One glance, and you know.

- 🟩 **Local-first.** Your metrics, layouts, and keys live in your browser's storage. Not our servers. We can't read what we never receive.
- 🟩 **Open source.** This repository will contain the full extension source at launch. Auditable, forkable, yours.
- 🟩 **No telemetry.** Zero analytics by default. The dashboard is for watching your numbers — not for anyone watching you.
- 🟨 **Connected sources.** `████████` — redacted until launch. (You can guess some of them.)
