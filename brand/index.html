04 / Discipline

The system works through restraint. The interface is an instrument, not a light show.

DO

  • Give green one job per view.
  • Use monospaced, tabular metrics.
  • Keep surfaces dark and green-tinted.
  • Use stepped motion for brand moments.

DON'T

  • Use green for body copy or wallpaper.
  • Use pure black, pure white, or green gradients.
  • Turn every control into a pill.
  • Animate the mark in print or routine chrome.
